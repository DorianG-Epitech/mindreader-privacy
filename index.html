Summary

Mindreader collects no personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising SDK and does not even request Internet access. Everything you enter stays on your phone, except your name and profile picture, which are shared directly with the nearby phones in a multi-phone game.

Who we are

Mindreader is an independent, non-commercial Android game published by Mindreader (France). Contact: open an issue on GitHub (issues are public, so please don't include personal information).

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, and is never sent to the developer or any server: player names, profile pictures (taken with your camera app or picked from your gallery, cropped to a small 128-pixel image), randomly generated avatars, custom cards, game settings (language, sound, enabled packs). It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Multi-phone games (Google Nearby Connections)

In “Several phones” mode, phones connect directly to each other using Google Nearby Connections (Bluetooth, Bluetooth Low Energy and Wi-Fi), without Internet. Only what the game needs is exchanged between the phones of the players in the same game: your name, your profile picture, your clues, custom cards used in the game, needle positions and scores. This data is kept in memory for the duration of the game and is not stored on a server. Nearby Connections is provided by Google Play services on your phone; Google's own privacy policy applies to Google Play services.

What we don't do

No Internet access, no data collection, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK (the ad banner in the app is a built-in joke with fake ads), no tracking, no in-app purchases, no sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Children

Mindreader is a party game intended for teenagers and adults (13+). It is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal data, no data from children is collected either.

Your rights

As no personal data is collected or processed by the developer, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You keep full control: you can edit or delete names, photos and custom cards in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For any question, open an issue on GitHub. You may also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Qui sommes-nous

Mindreader est un jeu Android indépendant et non commercial publié par Mindreader (France). Contact : ouvrir un ticket (issue) sur GitHub (les tickets sont publics : n'y mettez pas d'informations personnelles).

Données stockées sur votre appareil

Les données suivantes sont stockées uniquement dans l'espace privé de l'application sur votre téléphone, et ne sont jamais envoyées au développeur ni à un serveur : prénoms des joueurs, photos de profil (prises avec votre appli appareil photo ou choisies dans la galerie, réduites à une petite image de 128 pixels), avatars générés aléatoirement, cartes perso, réglages (langue, son, packs activés). Parties à plusieurs téléphones (Google Nearby Connections)

En mode « Plusieurs téléphones », les téléphones se connectent directement entre eux grâce à Google Nearby Connections (Bluetooth, Bluetooth basse consommation et Wi-Fi), sans Internet. Seul ce dont le jeu a besoin est échangé entre les téléphones des joueurs de la même partie : votre prénom, votre photo de profil, vos indices, les cartes perso utilisées, les positions d'aiguille et les scores. Ces données sont gardées en mémoire le temps de la partie et ne sont stockées sur aucun serveur. Nearby Connections est fourni par les services Google Play de votre téléphone ; les règles de confidentialité de Google s'appliquent aux services Google Play.
